About ACESO

Care that started on the field, now reaching further.

ACESO, the Active Care and Education in Sports and Outreach Foundation, began in August 2024 around sports injury prevention for student athletes. It has grown into a broader health nonprofit centered on biomedical research support, health education, and community health engagement.

Research

Zweistein
Innovation Lab

The student-led biomedical research initiative of ACESO. Focused on early-stage, exploratory research rather than clinical deployment or commercialization. A research and educational effort, not a clinical laboratory.

Focus

Neonatal life-support systems.

We study premature infant care and extracorporeal life-support, including neonatal ECMO and artificial placenta platforms. Roughly 1 in 10 babies worldwide are born prematurely, and preterm birth complications are a leading cause of death in children under five.

Source: World Health Organization →
